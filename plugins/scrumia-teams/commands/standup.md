---
description: Convene the project's standing roles — manager, business, tech, and any role another module ships — so they can be asked something. Starts no sprint.
argument-hint: [what to put to the team]
---

Load the `scrumia-standup` skill and follow it.

$ARGUMENTS

If arguments are present, that is what the team is convened about: put it to each enabled role and ask for its position within what that role owns. With none, convene them for a read of the current state.

Where two roles disagree, carry both positions over unchanged. Averaging them destroys the one thing convening a team produces.

Do not start a sprint. Running tickets is `sprint`, and launching it is a separate decision that belongs to the human.
