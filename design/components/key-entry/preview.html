key-entry

theme

One field that takes a whole qualified key. The source is typed as part of the answer, never picked beside it: the prefix set is open, and a picker could never produce the "no source stated" a refusal has to be able to fire on.

Three of the four states below are unreachable without script on the real page, which is why all four are rendered here rather than left to be clicked to.

1 — Resting: the option, no box

The entry is indistinguishable in anatomy from any other option in the shelf. The section's resting state holds no empty field.

not added — a key needs its source: local:, shared:, or owner/repo:

2 — Revealed, empty: asked, not yet answered

A revealed field nobody has typed into is unanswered, never refused. No dashed border, no gap line — painting an empty box as an error is how a composition starts reading as a form.

not added — a key needs its source: local:, shared:, or owner/repo:

3 — Revealed, answered: the decision landed

The enclosing option wears the --human wash every chosen option wears. Nothing about the field says "valid" — a decision that landed is reported by the artifacts, not by a green tick.

not added — a key needs its source: local:, shared:, or owner/repo:

4 — Refused: three channels, no colour

Dashed border — this system's mark for a decided absence, borrowed from the empty slot's leader. The --human wash withdrawn, because the decision did not land. And the message itself in the gap idiom. The refusal never quotes what was typed: it states the rule the visitor does not know, not the mistake they do.
