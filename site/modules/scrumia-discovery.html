💡 scrumia-discovery

Where an idea is questioned until it becomes work someone can execute.

slot · discovery version · 0.4.0

What it is responsible for

Owns the human-led half of the flow: an idea is challenged, scoped, and only then turned into issues carrying a verifiable criterion and a parent feature. Splits what is too large to execute as one ticket before anyone opens a branch.

What it refuses

It does not decide for you while the idea is still soft, and it does not hand over a ticket whose intent it had to guess.

The assumption it makes

Assumes a vague brief is far cheaper to fix before ticketing than after. Costs a step an urgent idea will always want to skip — and a project that leaves the slot empty should say so rather than improvise it.
