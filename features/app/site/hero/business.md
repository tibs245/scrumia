# Business rules — Hero

## Value

For a first-time visitor landing on the home page. It brings the product's
first impression: what ScrumIA is, stated plainly enough that the visitor
understands it and wants to know more rather than leaving. It doubles as an
SEO surface — the headline and lead copy are also what a search result shows
before anyone visits. It matters because every other section on the site is
read only if the hero earns the scroll past it. Measured: not instrumented
today — search impressions and time spent below the fold are not tracked.

Business parent: none beyond the epic. The copy decision itself is recorded
in `ux.md` and the ticket, not a dedicated rule here.
