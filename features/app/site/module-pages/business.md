# Business rules — Module pages

## Value

For a visitor evaluating one specific module rather than the marketplace as a
whole — someone who followed a link, a search result, or a mention of a
module by name. It brings that visitor a page of their own: what the module
does, what it depends on, how to install it — without wading through the
other eleven. It also gives the site its long-tail SEO surface: twelve
modules in two languages are twenty-four pages a search engine can index and
rank individually, rather than one marketplace page competing for every
query at once. It matters because a module a visitor cannot find is a module
they cannot adopt. Measured: not instrumented today — no per-module traffic
or search-ranking data is tracked.

Business parent: `features/business/modular-composition/` — what a module is
and what it owes. This feature only decides how one gets shown; it adds no
rule of its own.
