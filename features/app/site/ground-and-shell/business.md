# Business rules — Ground and shell

## Value

For every visitor, in either theme, either language, with or without
JavaScript. It brings the ground everything else on the site is read on: a
page that stays legible and complete no matter what the visitor's system or
preferences ask for, so a reader is never blocked by the shell before they
reach a feature's own content. It matters because every other feature on the
site is read through this one — an illegible or incomplete shell undermines
all of them at once, not just itself. Measured: not instrumented today — no
legibility or completion metric is tracked.

No Business parent — purely technical, per `business.md`'s App rule: this
feature carries no business rule of its own, only the rules that keep an
interface honest across two themes, two languages and a reader with
JavaScript switched off.
