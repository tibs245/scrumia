# Extends map — value

## Value

For a reader deciding whether ScrumIA can be bent to their project without being forked.
It brings the extension mechanism shown once, in the place where that decision is made —
the home page — rather than left to be inferred from the module list. It matters because
the site currently argues composability through slots alone, which answers *what can be
replaced* and leaves *what can be added* unanswered; a reader whose need is not a slot
concludes the answer is no. Measurable: `extends` appears twice in the entire home page
today and not once in the reference, which is the gap this section closes.

## The rule shown here belongs elsewhere

What a register is, what a directive is, what a contribution may and may not name, and
the order the table renders in — all of it is
`features/business/modular-composition/`'s. This feature renders it and states none of
it.

That boundary is the one this section is most likely to break, because a diagram is a
statement and a diagram drawn from memory is a second rendering of a rule. The figure is
drawn from the mechanism as that feature describes it, and when the mechanism changes the
figure is wrong — not stale, wrong, and the criteria treat it that way.

## What the section argues

One claim, and it is the one the slot section cannot make: **a project extends ScrumIA
without replacing anything and without forking anything.** A skill declares where it can
be extended; any module contributes; nothing is stored, so nothing goes stale.

The claim is carried by showing the mechanism, not by asserting it — `design/identity.md`
is the authority on that rule, and this section is the most tempting place on the site to
break it, because the mechanism is abstract and an assertion would be shorter.

## What it does not do

It does not teach someone to write a contribution. That is the reference's job and the
module's own documentation's; a home-page section that becomes a tutorial has stopped
being an argument. The section ends by pointing at where the writing is explained, and
stops.
